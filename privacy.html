Privacy Policy

Data controller

Synthetic Swarm (SAS) · contact@syntheticswarm.ai

Data we use

We may process your first and last name, professional contact details, public LinkedIn profile, and certain information related to your company’s activity or changes in circumstances.

Why we use it

We use this data to identify professional prospecting opportunities and provide relevant prospect profiles to our customers.

Who may receive the data

The data may be shared with our professional customers and with service providers necessary to operate Synthetic Swarm.

Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for its use and to meet our obligations.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, or object to its use.

Contact: contact@syntheticswarm.ai

GDPR compliance and B2B prospecting

Synthetic Swarm is designed to support B2B commercial prospecting in accordance with GDPR principles and the rules applicable to professional outreach.

Prospect profiles focus on professional information relevant to outreach, including the decision-maker’s identity and role, professional contact details, company information and relevant business changes.

We apply principles of data minimisation, professional relevance and the right to object. Synthetic Swarm data should be used only for B2B outreach that is relevant to the professional activity of the person contacted.

Individuals can exercise their rights of access, rectification, deletion and objection through our Opt-out page.
