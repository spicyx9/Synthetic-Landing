Who we are

Synthetic Swarm provides insurance brokers and agents with prospecting records about directors of French businesses, together with a dated signal.

Data controller: [[A COMPLETER : dénomination sociale du responsable du traitement]]. Address: [[A COMPLETER : adresse du responsable du traitement]]. Privacy contact: [[A COMPLETER : contact dédié aux données personnelles]].

Who this policy applies to

This policy covers visitors to syntheticswarm.ai, customers and service users, people who contact Synthetic Swarm, and business directors whose information appears in our prospecting database.

Service customers

Customers subscribe online to a monthly service paid by card through Stripe. Information needed to provide the service is processed to perform the contract; information that must be retained by law is processed to meet those legal obligations.

Business directors in the prospecting database

Data processed. The director’s identity (first and last name), professional contact details (mobile number, landline and email), public professional LinkedIn profile, and the detected signal and its date, explaining why a business contact may be relevant.

Sources. French public registers: [[A COMPLETER : liste exacte des sources]].

Purpose and legal basis. Compiling records and delivering them to customers relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, namely enabling business-to-business prospecting.

Balancing interests. The scope is limited to professional contact information: no sensitive data, no private-life data, and no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. A simple, free objection process is provided. [[A COMPLETER : analyse documentée de mise en balance de l’intérêt légitime et des droits des personnes]].

Transparency. This page makes information publicly available. Relying on collective notice under Article 14(5)(b) of the GDPR requires evidence that individual notice would involve disproportionate effort and appropriate measures to protect people’s rights. Public availability alone does not establish this exception. [[A COMPLETER : justification documentée des efforts disproportionnés et mesures de protection au titre de l’article 14.5.b]]. Without that justification, individual notice remains required.

Recipients. Subscribing business customers receive records for their own prospecting. They are responsible for their use of contact details and for complying with the rules applicable to their prospecting.

Retention. [[A COMPLETER : durée de conservation des données de la base de prospection]].
