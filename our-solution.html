Our solution

Know who to call. And why now.

Synthetic Swarm helps insurance professionals identify companies and executives whose situation has changed, with a concrete reason to contact them.

A list is not a reason to call.

The traditional list

“Here are companies you could call.” A name and a number do not tell you what has changed.

Synthetic Swarm

“Here are the companies to call now, and why.” The contact comes with a dated signal.

Spot the change that matters.

We continuously monitor changes at companies and among executives that may create a new insurance need. We then identify the relevant person and prepare a prospect profile.

How it works

1

Define your target

Profession, company size and geographic area.

2

Synthetic Swarm monitors the market

We detect relevant changes in circumstances.

3

Receive profiles ready to call

Each profile contains the contact, detected signal, its date and the reason to call now.

Which changes should you look for?

A new company, a first hire, a new establishment: these are illustrative situations to discuss for your target market. Confirm signal coverage with our team during your demo.
