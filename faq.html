FAQ

Your questions, clear answers.

The product, the data and the questions to ask before you start.

Product
How does it work in practice?

You give us your targeting criteria: profession, company size and geographic area. We continuously monitor for executives whose situation creates a new insurance need. Every week, you receive verified prospect profiles with the dated signal explaining why now is the right time to call.

Data
What information do I get?

The executive's first and last name, mobile number (and landline if available), email and LinkedIn profile. You also get the reason for the call: the change in circumstances we detected, with its date.

Signals
What exactly is a signal?

A dated change in circumstances that creates an insurance need. A self-employed professional who has just incorporated as a French SEL needs to review their social security status and personal protection cover. A company that has just hired its first employee needs group insurance. A tradesperson moving from sole trader status to a company needs ten-year liability insurance. You are not calling someone at random. You are calling someone with an issue to address this week.

Contact details
Is the phone number the executive's own?

Yes. We provide the executive's mobile number, not the switchboard. Where only a landline is available, it is clearly identified, and any record without a reachable contact method is replaced.

Targeting
Can I specify professions and my geographic area?

Yes. Your targeting criteria include profession, company size and geographic area.

Exclusivity
Do other clients receive the same records as me?

No. Each record delivered to you is reserved for you and is not shared with any other client.

Delivery
How often are records delivered?

You receive verified profiles every week, with the dated signal explaining why to call now.

Getting started
How soon will I receive my first delivery?

Less than 30 minutes after your criteria are approved.

Adjustments
What if the records do not match what I am looking for?

Contact us and we will adjust immediately. Our job is to help you target exactly the clients you want, not simply deliver volume.

Prospecting
Am I allowed to call these people?

Yes. Telephone prospecting between professionals in France remains permitted when the offer relates to the activity of the person being called. The prior-consent requirement introduced on August 11, 2026 applies to consumer telemarketing, not B2B.
