The same problem, experienced from two sides.

Ilan

Finding companies worth a call.

IESEG → Chief of Staff

As a Chief of Staff, Ilan had to find new customers. He spent a great deal of time identifying the right companies, understanding their circumstances and deciding which ones were worth a salesperson’s time.

The tools could find contacts. They were much less useful at explaining why a company should be contacted today.

Axel

Knowing where to focus his efforts.

emlyon → Entrepreneur

While building his first business, Axel was also looking for better ways to identify and qualify prospects. He tried different tools to find more customers and focus his efforts on the best opportunities.

He found the same problem: plenty of data and contacts, but little context to decide who to prioritize and when.

So we built it.

We started building Synthetic Swarm around one simple question: among all the companies a salesperson could call, which ones actually have a reason to be contacted now?
