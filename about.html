ABOUT

We built the tool we were looking for.

Two different experiences led us to the same problem: finding prospects is easy. Knowing which ones to contact at the right moment is much harder.

Axel CEO

Entrepreneur since 19 · emlyon business school

At 19, Axel started his first company and grew it into a book distribution group generating more than €1M in annual revenue.

After five years of finding, qualifying and winning customers himself, he structured the business so it could run with a manager and moved to the United States to build Synthetic Swarm.

Throughout those five years, he kept facing the same problem: plenty of potential prospects, but no tool telling him who to call first — and why now.

LinkedIn ↗

Ilan CTO

Finance · CNP Assurances · Chief of Staff

After studying finance, Ilan joined CNP Assurances as an Operational Risk Analyst. For two years, he built management tools, automated processes and worked on risk topics for senior leadership.

He later became Chief of Staff at Hyperstack, where he worked on financial and operational management, post-acquisition integration and the lead-conversion pipeline.

That is where he encountered the same problem directly: finding companies is easy; identifying the ones with a real reason to buy now is much harder.
